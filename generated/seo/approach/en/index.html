02 · Flight plan

From first signal to launch

Every project is different. The scope, schedule and deliverables are agreed together before production begins, then adjusted openly if priorities change.

01

Discover

We talk through your goals, audience, existing tools and constraints. Together we identify the core problem and what a useful outcome looks like.

02

Define the mission

We shape a proposal with scope, deliverables, responsibilities, estimated timing and budget, so everyone knows what is included.

03

Design the experience

We map key journeys and explore a visual direction. You review and give feedback at agreed checkpoints before build decisions are locked in.

04

Build and validate

We develop the approved experience, share progress and test key flows across devices. Questions and scope changes are discussed early.

05

Launch and hand over

We prepare the release, check the essentials and provide agreed documentation, access and handover support.

06

Support the next move

After launch, fixes, maintenance or further improvements can be agreed as a separate support arrangement.

02 · Flight plan

Ways to work together

Choose a useful starting point. We will agree the scope, timing and budget together before work begins.

01

Defined project

For a website, app or system with a clear goal. We agree the scope, deliverables and milestones up front, then design and build toward them.

02

Discovery first

For an idea that needs shaping before a build commitment. We clarify the problem, priorities and possible solution, then outline a practical next step.

03

Ongoing support

For improvements after launch. We agree the support tasks, cadence and response expectations separately, based on what your team needs.
